## 1. Headline findings
1. **Your Google Business Profile is your strongest asset, not the website.** From Utrecht (mobile), Busje komt zo is in the **Maps pack at #1 for "verhuizen utrecht" and "piano verhuizen", #2 for "verhuisbedrijf" and "verhuislift huren", #3 for "verhuisbedrijf utrecht" and #4 for "verhuizers utrecht"** (measured). The profile: **4.9 stars, 162 reviews, 88 photos, claimed, open 07:00–22:00 every day**.
2. **The website ranks mostly on page 2–3.** Nationally: #6 for "verhuizers utrecht", #9 "verhuizen utrecht", #21 "verhuisbedrijf utrecht", #36 "verhuisbedrijf amersfoort", #27 "nieuwegein", #26 "houten" (measured, 88 ranking keywords). From Utrecht itself, `/verhuisbedrijf-utrecht/` ranks **#10** for "verhuisbedrijf utrecht" and #11 for "verhuizers utrecht".
3. **`/verhuisbedrijf-utrecht/` is the page Google uses for the head term**, not the homepage. This **reverses** audit item O3: keep `/verhuisbedrijf-utrecht/` as the "verhuisbedrijf Utrecht" page. It is also the page the redesign cuts from about 1,750 to 514 words, so restoring its depth is the top content priority.
4. **The real competition is not who I listed first.** The domains that win your 25 money keywords (measured) are Student Verhuis Service, VBU, Snelle Jongens, VSN, verhuislifthurenutrecht.nl, Budget Verhuisservice and Truckit. Waaijenberg, AA Verhuizers and SW de Vries barely register.
5. **Your weak spots**:
   - Cost questions: 3,800+ searches a month combined; you rank nowhere.
   - Verhuislift: 4,400 a month; your blog post ranks #17–38.
   - Woningontruiming: 1,000 a month; you're not in the Maps pack.
   - Spoed: you're not in the Maps pack from Utrecht.
   - Zeist: not ranking at all, because the page is redirected.
6. **Backlink authority is low and falling**: 48 referring domains, many of them spam (spam score 37). About 120 backlinks were lost between March and July 2026, and the domain rank fell from 32 to 16 (measured).

## 4. What the top 3 rank for (measured: ranked keywords, top 100 by traffic)
### Student Verhuis Service: 1,607 keywords
- Traffic comes from the **homepage (Amsterdam focus)**, `/en/` (English, 3,132 estimated visits), **`/verhuismaterialen/verhuislift-huren/`** (#1 "verhuislift huren", 4,400 a month), **`/wat-kost-een-verhuizing/`** (#1–3 for every cost query), `/tijdelijke-opslag-verhuizing/`, `/woningontruiming/` (#2, 1,000 a month), and `/verhuisbedrijf-utrecht/` (#1 nationally).
- **Pattern**: one strong page per service with **a price in the title** ("Verhuislift huren? Vanaf €72…", "Woningontruiming regelen? Prijs binnen 5 minuten").
### VBU: 389 keywords
- About half its traffic comes **through its Google Business Profile** (`/?utm_source=GBP`, 46 keywords in Maps: "verhuizers", "verhuisservice", "verhuizen en opslag", "verhuisbedrijf offerte" …).
- Organic winners: homepage (#2 "verhuisbedrijf", 5,400 a month!), **`/m3-calculator/` (#3 "m3 berekenen", 1,900 a month)**, `/adreswijziging-doorgeven/` (#2 "verhuizing doorgeven utrecht"), `/huis-ontruimen-na-overlijden/`, `/kosten-verhuisbedrijf/`, `/hoeveel-verhuisdozen-nodig/` (#3, 320 a month), `/wasmachine-verhuizen/`.
- **Pattern**: GBP review volume plus simple tools and info posts.
### Snelle Jongens: 528 keywords
- A **national city-page network** (`/verhuisbedrijf-[stad]/`) plus `/verhuislift-huren/[stad]/` plus `/en/[stad]-moving-company/`. Thin template pages that still rank #1–3 in smaller towns.
- **Pattern**: city pages work when competition is low, which is also why your old city pages rank #11–19 in Eindhoven, Oss, Delft, Veenendaal and Zoetermeer.
